# FinMate / Finance tracker APP
Simple and smart personal finance tracker with budgeting and statistics

Finance Tracker helps you create accounts, categorize your income and expenses, track your balance, and plan your budget.  
With clear charts, detailed history, and flexible customization, you always know where your money goes and how much you have left.  

---

## ✨ Features
- **Accounts** – create and manage multiple accounts (cash, cards, etc.); hide accounts from the total balance if needed  
- **Categories** – add custom categories for expenses and income with icons and colors  
- **Transactions** – record expenses and income with amount, category, account, date, and optional comment as a custom title  
- **Statistics**  
  - Pie chart: expense distribution by categories for the selected period  
  - Bar chart: yearly income overview month by month  
- **Planning** – set planned expenses per category and monitor how much you’ve already spent vs. your budget  
- **History** – filter transactions by period, type (income/expense), or category  
- **Customization** – edit accounts and categories anytime, switch app language (English / Russian), choose currency (₽, $, €, BYN, ₸, ₴)  

---

## 💡 Benefits
- Full control over your money across different accounts  
- Clear understanding of your spending habits  
- Stay within budget thanks to planning and tracking  
- Flexibility: customize categories, currencies, and languages  
- Keep your finances organized with a simple and intuitive interface  

---

## 📖 How to use
1. **Create accounts**  
   Add your accounts (e.g., Cash, Bank card). You can hide accounts if you don’t want them to affect the total balance.  

2. **Set up categories**  
   Create categories for both expenses and income (e.g., Salary, Advance, Groceries).  

3. **Add transactions**  
   When adding a transaction, specify:  
   - amount  
   - category  
   - account  
   - date  
   - optional comment *(used as a custom title for the transaction)*  

4. **Track statistics**  
   - Pie chart: see expense distribution by categories for a chosen period  
   - Bar chart: view annual income statistics month by month  

5. **Plan your budget**  
   Create planned expenses for a period by category and track how much you’ve already spent compared to your limit.  

6. **Manage your data**  
   - Edit accounts and categories anytime  
   - Switch between languages (English / Russian)  
   - Choose currency (₽, $, €, BYN, ₸, ₴)  

7. **View balance and history**  
   - Home screen shows your total balance, balances per account, and recent transactions  
   - Full history: filter by period, type (income/expense), or category
